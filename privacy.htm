🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: January 2024

At Pen Tube, we take your privacy and data security extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI Creator-OS platform.

🛡️ Our Commitment to Your Privacy

We believe that your creative content belongs to you, and only you. Pen Tube is designed with privacy-first principles:

  • ✅ 100% Secure: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • ✅ Never Shared: We never sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties
  • ✅ Your Vault Only: Generated content is stored solely in your personal Creator Vault
  • ✅ Full Control: You can delete your data at any time

📊 What Data We Collect

1. Account Information

When you sign in with Google or email, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (if available)
  • Unique user ID (for authentication)

2. Generated Content

When you use our AI tools, we save:

  • Video titles and ideas you input
  • AI-generated thumbnail prompts
  • SEO keywords and tags
  • Script outlines
  • Thumbnail preview images

💾 How We Store Your Data

All your data is securely stored in Google Firebase, a trusted cloud platform with enterprise-grade security:

  • 🔐 Encryption: All data is encrypted using AES-256
  • 🌐 Secure Transfer: All communications use HTTPS/TLS
  • 🏢 Isolated Storage: Your data is stored in your personal Firestore document, isolated from other users
  • 📍 Data Residency: Data is stored in secure Google Cloud data centers

🚫 What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ We never use your content to train AI models
  • ❌ We never share your data with advertisers
  • ❌ We never sell your information to third parties
  • ❌ We never access your Creator Vault without your explicit permission
  • ❌ We never track your browsing behavior outside Pen Tube

🔑 Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your saved content in the Creator Vault
  • Download: Export your data as TXT or JSON files
  • Delete: Remove individual items or delete your entire account
  • Portability: Take your data with you if you leave

👥 Admin Access

Platform administrators (like Amila Kanishka) have limited access to:

  • Aggregate usage statistics (no personal data)
  • Platform health metrics
  • Support requests (only when you explicitly contact us)

Admins CANNOT:

  • ❌ View your personal Creator Vault
  • ❌ Access your generated content
  • ❌ See your private data

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

Pen Tube uses minimal cookies for:

  • Authentication (to keep you signed in)
  • Session management

We do NOT use:

  • ❌ Third-party tracking cookies
  • ❌ Advertising cookies
  • ❌ Analytics that identify you personally

🔒 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security:

  • 🛡️ Firebase Authentication with OAuth 2.0
  • 🔐 Firestore Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access
  • 🌐 HTTPS encryption for all data transfers
  • 📊 Regular security audits
  • 🚨 Incident response procedures

👶 Children's Privacy

Pen Tube is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notifications. Your continued use of Pen Tube after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

📧 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

  • 📧 Email: privacy@pentube.app
  • 💬 Discord: Join our community server
  • 🐦 Twitter: @PenTubeApp

🎯 Summary

Pen Tube is 100% free and 100% private. Your creative content is yours alone. We exist to empower creators, not exploit their data. Thank you for trusting us with your creative journey.
